# Finding a Therapist for a Preteen in Seattle & the Eastside

A single-page guide for parents of 11–13 year olds: clinicians, hospital programs and benefit platforms in Seattle and on the Eastside, checked for whether they see a 12-year-old, with filters for method, area, format, insurance (Aetna), age and license level. Includes a note on Washington's age-of-consent rules (RCW 71.34) and questions to ask on a first call.

Live: https://idvorkin-ai-tools.github.io/seattle-preteen-therapists/

Compiled from public practice pages and directories on 2026-10-10. Licenses are not verified. Not medical advice: verify licenses at the WA DOH provider credential search and coverage with your insurer.
